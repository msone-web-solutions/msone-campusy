## Erklärung

### Ein Gespräch hat drei Teile

Jedes Gespräch – am Bus, in der Klasse, im Urlaub – läuft nach demselben Muster: **eröffnen, weiterführen, beenden**. Wer die passenden Wendungen (phrases) kennt, wirkt sicher, auch wenn der Wortschatz noch klein ist.

### 1. Eröffnen (opening)

- Begrüßung: *Hi! / Hello! / Good morning. / Hey, how's it going?*
- Sich vorstellen: *I'm Lena. – Nice to meet you. – Nice to meet you too.*
- Small talk starten: *Lovely weather, isn't it? – Are you new here? – Do you come here often?*
- Fragen zur Person: *Where are you from? – What do you do? – Which school do you go to? – How old are you?*

### 2. Weiterführen (keeping it going)

Das Geheimnis guter Gespräche: **Interesse zeigen und nachfragen**.

- Nachfragen: *Really? – What do you mean? – Why? – And then? – Tell me more about it.*
- Interessen und Vorlieben: *What do you like doing in your free time? – Are you into football? – I'm keen on music. – I'm not really into sport.*
- Zustimmen und ablehnen: *I agree. – Me too. / Me neither. – I'm not sure about that. – I don't think so.*
- Meinung mit Begründung: *I think … because … – In my opinion … – If you ask me …*
- Höflich unterbrechen: *Sorry to interrupt, but … – Can I say something?*
- Wenn du etwas nicht verstanden hast: *Sorry, could you say that again? – What does „…“ mean? – Could you speak more slowly, please?*

Verknüpfe deine Sätze mit **and, but, because, so, then** – das klingt sofort flüssiger: *I like the film because it's funny, but the ending is a bit sad.*

### 3. Beenden (closing)

- Das Ende ankündigen: *Well, I have to go now. – It was nice talking to you. – Anyway, I'd better get going.*
- Verabschieden: *See you (later / tomorrow)! – Take care! – Bye! – Have a nice day!*
- Kontakt halten: *Let's keep in touch. – Can I have your number?*

### Bitten, Wünsche, Entschuldigungen

- Bitten: *Could you help me, please? – Would you mind opening the window?*
- Wünsche: *I'd like a coke, please. – I'd love to come.*
- Entschuldigung: *I'm sorry. – Sorry, that was my fault. – Excuse me!* (um jemanden anzusprechen)
- Gefühle: *I'm really happy about … – I'm a bit worried about … – That's annoying.*

Merke: *Sorry* = Entschuldigung für etwas · *Excuse me* = Ansprechen oder Vorbeigehen.

## Hefteintrag

**Gespräche führen – useful phrases**

**Eröffnen:** *Hi! Nice to meet you. – Nice to meet you too. · Where are you from? · What do you do? · Lovely weather, isn't it?*

**Weiterführen:**
- nachfragen: *Really? – Why? – Tell me more. – What do you mean?*
- Vorlieben: *I'm into … / I'm keen on … / I'm not really into …*
- Meinung: *I think … because … – In my opinion … – I agree. / I don't think so.*
- unterbrechen: *Sorry to interrupt, but …* · nicht verstanden: *Could you say that again?*
- verknüpfen: **and, but, because, so, then**

**Beenden:** *Well, I have to go now. – It was nice talking to you. – See you! – Take care! – Let's keep in touch.*

**Bitten/Wünsche:** *Could you …, please? – Would you mind …ing? – I'd like …*
**Sorry** = Entschuldigung · **Excuse me** = ansprechen
