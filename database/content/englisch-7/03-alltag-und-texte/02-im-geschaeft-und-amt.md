## Erklärung

### Das Muster ist immer gleich

Ob Bäckerei, Bahnhof oder Arztpraxis: Solche Gespräche haben eine feste Struktur. **Begrüßung – Anliegen – Nachfragen – Abschluss.** Und sie sind höflicher als Gespräche unter Freunden: viel *please, thank you, could, would*.

### Im Geschäft (at the shop)

- Verkäufer: *Can I help you? – Are you looking for anything in particular?*
- Du: *I'm just looking, thanks. – I'm looking for a jumper. – Do you have this in a smaller size / in blue?*
- Preis: *How much is this? – How much does it cost? – That's a bit expensive.*
- Anprobieren: *Can I try it on? – Where are the changing rooms?*
- Kaufen: *I'll take it. – Can I pay by card? – Here you are. – Here's your change. – Would you like a receipt?*

### Am Bahnhof / im Café

- *A return ticket to Manchester, please. – Single or return? – Which platform does the train leave from? – When is the next train to …?*
- *Can I have a coffee and a sandwich, please? – Eat in or take away? – Anything else? – That's £5.50, please.*

### Beim Arzt (at the doctor's)

- Termin: *I'd like to make an appointment with Dr Smith. – Is it urgent? – Can you come in at 10 tomorrow?*
- Beschwerden: *What's the matter? / What seems to be the problem? – I've got a headache / a sore throat / a temperature. – My leg hurts. – I feel sick.*
- Arzt: *Take these tablets twice a day. – Stay in bed for two days. – You should drink a lot.*

### Sich beschweren (making a complaint)

Höflich, aber bestimmt:
1. Problem nennen: *Excuse me, I bought this T-shirt yesterday and there's a hole in it.*
2. Beleg zeigen: *Here's the receipt.*
3. Lösung fordern: *I'd like my money back. / Could I exchange it, please?*
4. Bei Ablehnung: *I'm sorry, but that's not good enough. Could I speak to the manager?*

### Auskünfte einholen und geben

- Fragen: *Excuse me, could you tell me where the post office is? – How do I get to the station? – What time does the museum close?*
- Antworten: *Go straight on, then turn left at the traffic lights. – It's on your right, next to the bank. – It closes at 6.*

## Hefteintrag

**Gespräche in Institutionen**

Struktur: Begrüßung – Anliegen – Nachfragen – Abschluss · höflich: *please, could, would, thank you*

**Shop:** *Can I help you? – I'm just looking. / I'm looking for … – Do you have this in a smaller size? – How much is it? – Can I try it on? – I'll take it. – Can I pay by card?*

**Station / café:** *A return ticket to …, please. – Which platform? – Can I have …, please? – Eat in or take away?*

**Doctor's:** *make an appointment · What's the matter? – I've got a headache / a sore throat. – My leg hurts. – Take these tablets twice a day.*

**Complaint:** Problem nennen (*there's a hole in it*) → Beleg (*here's the receipt*) → Lösung (*I'd like my money back / could I exchange it?*) → *Could I speak to the manager?*

**Directions:** *Could you tell me where … is? – Go straight on, turn left at the lights, it's on your right.*
