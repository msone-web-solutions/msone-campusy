At the shop, the station, the doctor's: Gespräche in Institutionen.

Hello! Heute geht es um Alltagsgespräche mit Fremden: Einkaufen, Fahrkarten, Beschwerden, Arzttermin.

Ob Bäckerei, Bahnhof oder Arztpraxis: Solche Gespräche haben eine feste Struktur. Begrüßung, Anliegen, Nachfragen, Abschluss. Und sie sind höflicher als Gespräche unter Freunden: viel please, thank you, could und would.

Im Geschäft. Der Verkäufer fragt: Can I help you? Are you looking for anything in particular? Du sagst: I'm just looking, thanks. Oder: I'm looking for a jumper. Do you have this in a smaller size? In blue? Der Preis: How much is this? That's a bit expensive. Anprobieren: Can I try it on? Where are the changing rooms? Kaufen: I'll take it. Can I pay by card? Here you are. Here's your change. Would you like a receipt?

Am Bahnhof: A return ticket to Manchester, please. Single or return? Which platform does the train leave from? When is the next train? Im Café: Can I have a coffee and a sandwich, please? Eat in or take away? Anything else? That's five pounds fifty, please.

Beim Arzt. Termin: I'd like to make an appointment with Doctor Smith. Is it urgent? Beschwerden: What's the matter? What seems to be the problem? I've got a headache, a sore throat, a temperature. My leg hurts. I feel sick. Der Arzt: Take these tablets twice a day. Stay in bed for two days. You should drink a lot.

Sich beschweren, höflich, aber bestimmt. Erstens das Problem nennen: Excuse me, I bought this T-shirt yesterday and there's a hole in it. Zweitens den Beleg zeigen: Here's the receipt. Drittens eine Lösung fordern: I'd like my money back. Could I exchange it, please? Und bei Ablehnung: I'm sorry, but that's not good enough. Could I speak to the manager?

Auskünfte einholen: Excuse me, could you tell me where the post office is? How do I get to the station? What time does the museum close? Und geben: Go straight on, then turn left at the traffic lights. It's on your right, next to the bank. It closes at six.

Fassen wir zusammen: Feste Struktur, höfliche Formen mit could und would. Im Shop: I'm looking for, try on, I'll take it. Beim Arzt: appointment, What's the matter, I've got a. Beschwerde: Problem, Beleg, Lösung.

Jetzt bist du dran. Hefteintrag abschreiben, dann zum Test. Good luck!
